How it works

Four steps, no willpower required

Parsly runs as a loop: capture, box, focus, check in.

📝

Turn a brain dump into a task list

Paste your notes or scan handwritten notes and a whiteboard with your camera. On-device text recognition and Apple Intelligence sort the mess into a reviewable list of tasks with suggested durations — nothing you paste or scan ever leaves your device.

⏱️

Every task gets a box

Mark a task one-off or routine, set how long it should take, and tap into focus. A ring counts down the whole time, so no task quietly runs long.

✅

The timer checks in on you

When the block ends, Parsly asks if you actually finished — "Did you get it done?" Say yes and you get a small reward screen, a reason to step away before diving into the next thing.

🔒

Nothing leaves your phone

Tasks, timers, and notes are stored on-device with Apple's SwiftData framework. There's no account to create, no server, and nothing to sync.

Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 09/15/2026

Parsly ("the app") is developed by L. Dante Guarin ("we," "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

What stays on your device

All of your tasks, time blocks, focus sessions, and settings are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. We do not operate a server, and none of this information is transmitted to us or to any third party.

On-device AI features

Parsly's "Tasks from summary" feature uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models (Apple Intelligence) and on-device Vision text recognition to turn pasted or scanned notes into tasks. This processing happens entirely on your device — the text you paste or the photos you scan are never sent to us, to Apple's servers, or to any other party.

Advertising

Parsly shows banner ads served by Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC, to support free use of the app. AdMob may collect device identifiers and use them, with your permission, to show more relevant ads. You will be asked for App Tracking Transparency permission before this happens, and you can decline at any time. You can review Google's privacy practices at policies.google.com/privacy. Purchasing the ad-free subscription removes these ads.

In-app purchases

Ad-free subscriptions ($1.99/month or $15.99/year) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Notifications

If you allow it, Parsly sends a local notification when a focus timer completes. These notifications are generated on your device and are not sent through any external service.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties, other than the ad and payment processing described above, which is handled directly by Google and Apple respectively under their own privacy policies.

Children's privacy

Parsly is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this same URL.
