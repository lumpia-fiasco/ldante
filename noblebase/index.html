    [ 01 ] The instrument

    Astrolabe starts with the people who actually paid you. Upload your customer list and it enriches every record with real, licensed consumer data — the demographic, behavioral, and interest signals your CRM can't see — then reads it back as practical marketing intelligence: who your buyers really are, where to reach them, and what to say. You could ask an AI who your customers are and get a confident guess; Astrolabe doesn't guess. It works from your real list, and uses AI only for the last step — making the findings readable.

    We built it because every campaign decision is downstream of a customer-understanding question. Astrolabe answers it the same day — grounded in your real customers, not a model's best guess — so we can act on it mid-engagement.

    [ 04 ] The ongoing reading

    An instrument you return to.

    Your customer base doesn't hold still. Every campaign you run changes who you attract, and churn builds quietly long before revenue feels it. A single reading tells you where you stand today — repeat readings tell you which direction you're moving.

    Churn watch.

    Upload the same list on a cadence and every customer is classified — new, retained, churned, or reactivated — so you see who's drifting toward the exit while there's still time to win them back.

    Retention playbooks.

    Each new reading comes with a retention playbook: which segments are at risk, what they have in common, and specific, prioritized actions to keep them.

    Audience drift.

    Every cohort carries its own demographic profile — see exactly who you're losing and who you're attracting, not just the counts.

    Fresh lookalikes.

    Generate new prospect audiences matched to your best current buyers, exported for Meta, Google, TikTok, Klaviyo, and more.
