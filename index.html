My design principles

Intention

I chase the business problem behind the design ask, not just the ask itself. The distinction often leads somewhere more valuable.

Insight

Usage and behavior tell me more than what people say they want. I look for the signals that reveal what's really happening — and what might be possible next.

Scale

Good design scales. I build with growth in mind because what works today may not work tomorrow — and today's customers aren't necessarily tomorrow's customers.

Connection

I build to connect. People, processes, systems — and ultimately, people to progress, accomplishment, and delight.
